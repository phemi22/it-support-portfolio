# Day 18 Lab: Microsoft Teams Microphone Not Working

## Priority
P2 - High

## Issue
User's microphone does not work during Microsoft Teams meetings, but speakers work normally.

## First Checks
1. Confirm Microsoft Teams is using the correct microphone in **Settings → Devices**.
2. Confirm Windows has permission to access the microphone.
3. Test the microphone in another application (Voice Recorder or Sound Settings).

## Action Taken
- Selected the correct microphone in Teams.
- Verified microphone permissions in Windows Settings.
- Restarted Microsoft Teams and asked the user to sign in again.

## Resolution
User tested the microphone in a Teams meeting and confirmed it was working.

Closed
